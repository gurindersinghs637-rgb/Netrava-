## 1. Clinical & Ophthalmic Grading Standards

- **International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) Disease Severity Scale:**
  - Standardized scale created by the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) and International Council of Ophthalmology (ICO).
  - Guides the 5-stage classification (0: No DR, 1: Mild, 2: Moderate, 3: Severe, 4: Proliferative).
- **Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study (ETDRS) 4-2-1 Rule:**
  - Severe NPDR defined by any one of: severe intraretinal microvascular abnormalities (IRMA) in $\ge 1$ quadrant, venous beading in $\ge 2$ quadrants, or blot hemorrhages in all 4 quadrants.
